## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. The **Hoysala One** app is used by 5.2 million customers and
carries a 4.3 Play Store rating; 74% of the bank's retail transactions are now
app-first, and the app ships in 11 languages.

## The role

The mobile app is Hoysala's primary branch — and it competes for the same thumb
space as PhonePe and Google Pay. As Product Manager for the Payments & Daily
Banking domain in Hoysala One (2 squads: Android/iOS/backend, ~14 people), you own
outcomes for how customers pay, get paid, and understand their money — from UPI
(AutoPay, Lite, RuPay credit on UPI) and payment requests to money-management
insights built on Account Aggregator data. You will run discovery and delivery
end-to-end and be measured on activation, task success and NPS, not feature count.

## What you'll do

- Own the roadmap and OKRs for the Payments & Daily Banking domain in the app.
- Run continuous discovery: customer interviews (largely in Kannada, Hindi and
  Tamil), funnel analysis, painted-door and A/B experiments (we use Amplitude,
  CleverTap and an in-house experimentation platform).
- Ship iteratively with your squads: write crisp problem statements, slice scope,
  make evidence-based trade-offs.
- Exploit the Account Aggregator framework to build money-management features that
  wallet competitors can't easily copy.
- Balance innovation with the realities of a bank: NPCI circular timelines, RBI
  digital-lending and payment-security guidelines, fraud patterns (mule accounts,
  social-engineering scams) and vernacular usability.
- Align stakeholders across Fraud, Compliance, Operations and Branch Banking.

## What you bring (must-haves)

- 5+ years of product management on consumer digital products, of which 2+ on a
  high-traffic mobile app.
- A demonstrated experimentation practice: you can point to decisions changed by
  A/B tests or research you ran.
- Fluency with product analytics tools (Amplitude, CleverTap, Mixpanel or similar)
  and the statistics to interpret them honestly.
- Experience shipping with in-house engineering squads in an agile setting.
- Excellent stakeholder communication in fluent professional English.

## Nice to have

- Payments, banking or fintech domain knowledge (UPI ecosystem, Account
  Aggregator, video-KYC, digital-lending guidelines).
- Kannada, Hindi or Tamil language skills (customer research is largely
  vernacular).
- Experience designing for first-time internet users / vernacular-first users.
- A technical or data background.
