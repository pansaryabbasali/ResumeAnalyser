## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. Our Treasury & Markets division runs trading books in government
securities, interest-rate derivatives and FX from the Mumbai dealing room, with the
quant and technology teams based in Bengaluru.

## The role

The Market & Counterparty Risk Models team (11 quants) owns the models the bank
uses to measure and capitalize market risk: VaR/Expected Shortfall for the trading
book under RBI's market-risk framework (including the FRTB-aligned draft
guidelines), IRRBB metrics for the banking book per the RBI circular, and
counterparty exposure models for the derivatives portfolio. As a Quantitative
Researcher you will develop, backtest and defend these models — to the desk, to
Model Validation, and to RBI inspection teams.

## What you'll do

- Develop and maintain VaR/ES models for the trading book (G-Sec, SDL, OIS, FX),
  including risk-factor modeling, P&L attribution tests and non-modellable risk
  factor treatment under the FRTB-aligned parallel run.
- Design and run backtesting frameworks; investigate breaches and drive
  remediation.
- Build and improve pricing and sensitivity models for linear rates, FX and
  vanilla-option products used in risk calculations, consistent with FIMMDA
  valuation norms.
- Implement research code in Python (production handover in C++ together with the
  quant development team).
- Document models to internal model-governance standards and present to Model
  Validation, ALCO and RBI inspection teams.
- Partner with Treasury on IRRBB scenario design (EVE/NII sensitivity under the
  prescribed shocks).

## What you bring (must-haves)

- M.Sc./M.Tech or PhD in a quantitative discipline (mathematics, physics,
  statistics, econometrics, quantitative finance or similar).
- Strong grounding in probability theory, stochastic processes and statistics.
- Working knowledge of derivatives pricing (discounting, curve construction,
  Black-Scholes-type models, Greeks).
- Strong Python; ability to read and contribute to C++ is a plus we test for.
- Ability to explain quantitative results to non-quant stakeholders in fluent
  professional English.

## Nice to have

- Direct experience with the RBI market-risk framework, FRTB (IMA or SA), IRRBB, or
  counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR, internal EPE models).
- Experience in a bank's treasury, risk or model-validation quant team, or a
  rates/FX quant role at a primary dealer.
- Experience presenting models during RBI inspections or to ALCO.
- FRM/CQF; publications or open-source work in quantitative finance.
