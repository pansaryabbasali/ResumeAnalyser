## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. Under **Project Garuda** (2025–2028) — our modernization of core
banking, payments and the data/AI platform — Technology at Hoysala is ~2,400 people
working in cross-functional product teams at the Hoysala Digital Centre in
Bellandur, Bengaluru, and our Chennai engineering hub.

## The role

Our Payments Platform processes over 21 million UPI transactions a day alongside
IMPS, NEFT and RTGS — with festive-season and IPL-final peaks running at three times
the daily average. Under Project Garuda we are replacing batch-oriented payment
services with an event-driven, cloud-native platform, keeping pace with NPCI's
compliance calendar, and completing the ISO 20022 transition for RTGS/NEFT
connectivity. As a Senior Backend Engineer you will design and build the services at
the heart of this platform, in a team of eight engineers that owns its services
end-to-end — you build it, you run it.

## What you'll do

- Design, build and operate JVM microservices for UPI, IMPS and NEFT/RTGS payment
  flows, from initiation through reconciliation.
- Deliver NPCI compliance and feature mandates (UPI Lite, AutoPay, RuPay credit on
  UPI) on unforgiving external deadlines.
- Drive the ISO 8583 and ISO 20022 message-transformation services to full
  production coverage.
- Work with Kafka-based event streaming as the backbone of the platform.
- Own non-functional requirements: idempotency, exactly-once semantics,
  back-pressure, four-nines availability, and RBI incident-reporting timelines.
- Participate in the team's on-call rotation and continuously improve runbooks.
- Coach mid-level engineers through design and code review.

## What you bring (must-haves)

- 5+ years of professional backend engineering experience on the JVM (Java and/or
  Kotlin) in high-volume production environments.
- Strong experience with Spring Boot, Kafka and PostgreSQL/Oracle.
- Hands-on experience running containerized workloads on Kubernetes in a public
  cloud (we run on AWS).
- A track record of designing distributed systems for correctness under failure
  (idempotency, retries, outbox patterns).
- Fluent professional English.

## Nice to have

- Payments domain experience: UPI switch or TPAP integrations, IMPS/NEFT/RTGS,
  ISO 8583, ISO 20022 (pacs/camt), or card processing.
- Kotlin in production.
- Experience in a bank, PSP or fintech operating under RBI/NPCI regulation.
- Familiarity with RBI master directions on digital payment security controls.
