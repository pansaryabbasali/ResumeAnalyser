## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. Under **Project Garuda** (2025–2028) — our modernization of core
banking, payments and the data/AI platform — Technology at Hoysala is ~2,400 people
working in cross-functional product teams at the Hoysala Digital Centre in
Bellandur, Bengaluru, and our Chennai engineering hub.

## The role

The Platform Reliability team runs the Kubernetes-based internal platform
("Bastion") on which 140+ product teams deploy. Availability of this platform is
availability of the bank: mobile banking, UPI and the branch network all run on it —
and the regulator notices when a bank's digital channels go down. As an SRE you will
raise the reliability bar — SLOs, capacity, incident response, and the automation
that keeps toil down — while the platform doubles in size under Project Garuda.

## What you'll do

- Define and operationalize SLOs/error budgets with product teams.
- Build and maintain infrastructure-as-code (Terraform) for our AWS and on-prem
  Kubernetes estates, within RBI data-localisation requirements.
- Extend our observability stack (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, OpenTelemetry).
- Automate away recurring operational work in Go or Python.
- Join a 24/7 on-call rotation across Bengaluru and Chennai; lead blameless
  post-incident reviews with RBI-reportable incidents handled to the clock.
- Run RBI-mandated DR drills and chaos/failover exercises for critical payment
  systems, with audit-ready evidence.

## What you bring (must-haves)

- 4+ years in SRE, DevOps or platform engineering roles running production systems.
- Deep working knowledge of Kubernetes and at least one major cloud (AWS preferred).
- Solid Terraform experience and Linux fundamentals.
- Proficiency scripting/programming in Go or Python.
- Hands-on experience with Prometheus-style monitoring and incident management.
- Fluent professional English.

## Nice to have

- Experience in banking, NBFC or another regulated, high-availability environment;
  familiarity with RBI IT outsourcing and cyber-resilience directions.
- Kafka, Vault, service-mesh (Istio/Linkerd) operations.
- CKA/CKS certification.
- Experience introducing SLOs to an organization for the first time.
