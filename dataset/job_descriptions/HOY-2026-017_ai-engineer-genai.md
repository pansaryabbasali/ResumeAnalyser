## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. Under **Project Garuda** (2025–2028) — our modernization of core
banking, payments and the data/AI platform — Technology at Hoysala is ~2,400 people
working in cross-functional product teams at the Hoysala Digital Centre in
Bellandur, Bengaluru, and our Chennai engineering hub.

## The role

The GenAI Center of Excellence (est. 2024, now 14 people) takes LLM applications
from idea to supervised production inside a bank — with the evaluation, guardrails
and audit trail that a regulated environment demands, aligned with RBI's FREE-AI
recommendations and the DPDP Act. Live use cases include a contact-center assistant
grounded in policy documents (with Hindi and Kannada query support), KYC-file
summarization, and developer productivity tooling. As an AI Engineer you will build
and harden these systems and the shared platform underneath them.

## What you'll do

- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines over the bank's document
  estate: ingestion, chunking, embedding, retrieval tuning, grounded generation
  with citations.
- Design and automate evaluation: golden sets, LLM-as-judge with human calibration,
  regression gates in CI before any prompt or model change ships — including
  vernacular (Hindi/Kannada/Tamil) query sets.
- Implement guardrails (input/output filtering, PII redaction under the DPDP Act,
  groundedness checks) and the logging needed for model risk management.
- Integrate models via our internal model gateway (hosted LLM APIs and open-weight
  models on our own GPUs); optimize for cost and latency.
- Ship production Python services (FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions).
- Pair with use-case teams across the bank to take pilots to production.

## What you bring (must-haves)

- 3+ years of professional software or ML engineering experience in Python.
- Hands-on production experience with LLM applications — RAG, structured
  output/function calling, prompt and context design (not only notebooks or POCs).
- Experience with vector stores (e.g. pgvector, OpenSearch, Chroma, Pinecone) and
  embedding models.
- Demonstrated approach to evaluating LLM systems beyond "it looks good" — golden
  sets, metrics, regression testing.
- Solid engineering fundamentals: testing, CI/CD, containers, code review.
- Fluent professional English.

## Nice to have

- Experience in BFSI or another regulated industry; familiarity with RBI's FREE-AI
  framework, DPDP Act obligations, or model risk management.
- Indic-language NLP experience (transliteration handling, code-mixed queries,
  Indic embeddings).
- Agent/tool-use architectures in production; open-weight model serving (vLLM).
- MLOps stack experience (MLflow, model registries, feature stores).
- Contributions to open-source AI tooling.
