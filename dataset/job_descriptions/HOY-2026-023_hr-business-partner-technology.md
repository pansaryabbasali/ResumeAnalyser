## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. Under **Project Garuda** (2025–2028) — our modernization of core
banking, payments and the data/AI platform — Technology at Hoysala is ~2,400 people
working in cross-functional product teams at the Hoysala Digital Centre in
Bellandur, Bengaluru, and our Chennai engineering hub.

## The role

Project Garuda is reshaping the Technology organisation: new teams, new roles, new
skills, insourcing from system-integrator vendors, and the phase-out of legacy
functions — in a Bengaluru talent market where product companies and GCCs compete
for the same engineers. As HR Business Partner for Technology you are the strategic
people-partner for two engineering directorates (~450 employees): you advise
directors and engineering managers on organisational design, workforce and
succession planning, complex individual cases, attrition, and the people side of
the transformation.

## What you'll do

- Partner with Technology directors on org design, role architecture and strategic
  workforce planning for the Garuda transition, including vendor-to-bank
  insourcing (rebadging) of engineering roles.
- Guide managers through complex employee-relations cases (performance improvement
  plans, disciplinary inquiries, long-term illness) compliant with the Karnataka
  Shops & Establishments Act and the bank's HR policy framework.
- Support statutory compliance in partnership with the compliance team: POSH Act
  processes (including ICC case support), PF/gratuity/ESI questions, contract-
  workforce governance.
- Use people analytics (attrition, engagement, internal mobility, pay-equity data)
  to ground advice in evidence — attrition in our technology population is the
  single biggest people-risk to Garuda.
- Run the annual talent, performance and reward cycles for your client groups,
  including campus (NIT/IIT/IIIT) and lateral onboarding cohorts.
- Coach engineering managers, many of whom are first-time people leaders.

## What you bring (must-haves)

- 6+ years of progressive HR experience of which 3+ as an HR Business Partner or
  HR Manager for a substantial client group.
- Demonstrable working knowledge of Indian employment law in practice (Karnataka
  S&E Act, POSH Act, PF/ESI/gratuity, contract labour) and disciplinary-inquiry
  processes.
- Experience supporting reorganisations, insourcing/rebadging or large change
  programs.
- A data-driven working style — comfortable building an argument from HR analytics.
- Professional fluency in English; Kannada and/or Hindi strongly preferred.

## Nice to have

- Experience partnering with technology/engineering organisations or GCCs.
- Darwinbox or Workday experience.
- Experience in banking, NBFC or another regulated sector.
- Certification in coaching or organisational development; SHRM/CIPD credentials.
