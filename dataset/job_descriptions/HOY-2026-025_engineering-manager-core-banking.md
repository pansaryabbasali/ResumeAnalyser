## About Hoysala Bank

Hoysala Bank Ltd. is an old-generation private-sector bank headquartered in
Bengaluru, founded in 1927. The bank serves 14 million customers through 830+
branches across 14 states, with total business of about ₹3.6 lakh crore and some
21,500 employees. Under **Project Garuda** (2025–2028) — our modernization of core
banking, payments and the data/AI platform — Technology at Hoysala is ~2,400 people
working in cross-functional product teams at the Hoysala Digital Centre in
Bellandur, Bengaluru, and our Chennai engineering hub.

## The role

The heart of Project Garuda: moving Hoysala's core banking capabilities — accounts,
ledger, product engine — off a 20-year-old heavily customised legacy CBS estate
(with its surrounding PL/SQL and COBOL batch landscape) onto a cloud-native target
architecture, without a single day of disruption to 14 million customers, and with
RBI watching digital-channel outages across the industry closely. You will lead
three teams (22 engineers across Bengaluru and Chennai) that own the
account-and-ledger domain: the new services, the strangler-pattern migration, and
the coexistence layer that keeps legacy and cloud consistent during the multi-year
transition.

## What you'll do

- Lead, grow and coach three cross-functional teams (~22 engineers, mixed
  seniority, two cities); hire with intent in a brutal talent market and develop
  your engineers.
- Own delivery of the account/ledger migration roadmap: sequencing, risk
  management, dependency management with 15+ consuming systems.
- Drive engineering quality: you are accountable for the reliability of both the
  legacy and target stacks during coexistence.
- Manage senior stakeholders: program leadership, Operations, Risk, and the
  system-integrator vendor relationship (AMC and change-request commercials).
- Balance transformation work against run-the-bank demands within a fixed budget.
- Shape the target operating model as legacy workloads wind down, including
  reskilling paths for CBS-customisation engineers.

## What you bring (must-haves)

- 8+ years in software engineering, of which 3+ years managing engineering teams
  (including managing at least ~8 engineers and hiring experience).
- Demonstrated delivery of a large migration or modernization program in a complex
  estate (core systems, high-integrity data, many consumers).
- Enough technical depth to challenge designs: distributed systems, data
  consistency, API/event integration patterns.
- Experience with senior stakeholder and vendor/SI management.
- Fluent professional English.

## Nice to have

- Banking or NBFC experience; familiarity with RBI IT examinations and change-risk
  governance.
- Legacy CBS coexistence experience (Finacle/BaNCS/Flexcube customisation
  landscapes alongside cloud services, CDC-based sync).
- Experience leading distributed teams across cities.
- Experience with strangler-fig migrations and event-driven architectures.
